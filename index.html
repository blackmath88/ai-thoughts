AI Thoughts

Essays on how AI systems should be built when intelligence is a capability, not the centre.

What this is

These essays argue for software that calls on intelligence where it is needed, instead of software rebuilt around an agent. They cover semantic computation, memory as reconstructable knowledge, and what control over AI can mean when the frontier moves every month.

Method

  1. Look back first. Every argument starts from history: someone has faced this problem before.
  2. Explain, then simulate. The text makes the case; the figures let you push on it.
  3. Label what is illustrative. Figures are deterministic and invented for explanation, and say so.
  4. End each piece open. Every essay closes on the question it could not settle.
